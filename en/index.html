About the holding

Since 1990 we have pursued the same mission

Today the holding's companies provide regional, nationwide and international transport services with 80 buses, more than 100 semi-trailer units and all services for motorists in the AUTOCENTRUM area.

Besides the logistics centre you will find here an inspection station for all vehicle types, a CNG filling station, car repair shops, tyre shops, shops with spare parts and tyres for cars and trucks, tachograph servicing and calibration, a car wash and a training centre for practical teaching of car mechanics. For visitors, our restaurant offers popular home-style cooking. In 2018 we also opened a new inspection station in Břeclav.
