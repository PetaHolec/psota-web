Company history

Passenger transport Lines and transport Services Company history Careers Fuel prices Conditions of carriage Contacts

From one small AVIA truck to a modern fleet of Scania, Mercedes-Benz and Iveco. Take a ride through our journey.

  1. 1990

    The roots of the history of today's company reach back to 1990, when Karel Psota Sr. founded the company Karel Psota Autoexpres. It started operating with a single bus of the type Škoda ŠD-11.

  2. 1991

    In 1991 another bus was added and lines to Brno and Prague were introduced, so far with only one connection. The then base for parking the fleet was, until 1994, in the Zenza company's premises on I. P. Pavlova street in Znojmo.

  3. 1992

    The first turning point came in 1992, when a contract was signed and regular transport for the Dukovany nuclear power plant was started. The company grew by another four buses and more employees, and a transport office for advance ticket sales with administrative facilities was opened at Rudoleckého street no. 25 in Znojmo. Transport on the regional lines Znojmo – Hatě and Znojmo – Miroslav – M. Krumlov was started without any subsidies.

  4. 1994

    Because of the growth of the fleet and the need to solve the service facilities, in 1994 the technical base of Agrostav, a.s. in Dobšice near Znojmo was rented. There, in spacious workshops, regular inspections and repairs of buses could be carried out without problems. For our own needs a fuel station and a washing facility for buses and trucks were put into operation. This site still serves our needs to a limited extent today.

  5. 1996

    The number of buses kept growing while the fleet was being renewed with new modern vehicles. In 1996 their number reached 20. That year also became the year of foundation of today's Znojemská dopravní společnost – PSOTA, s.r.o. Transport was extended with the line Znojmo – Čížov and another growth in occasional transport was recorded. The first buses powered by natural gas began to run.

  6. 1998

    In 1998 the fuel station was opened to the public, including other services for motorists. The company's administration moved from Rudoleckého street to newly rented office space also located in the Agrostav area in Dobšice.

  7. 2003

    Another turning point was the year 2003. Until then five regular lines were operated, 3 subsidised and 2 unsubsidised. In a tender the company was awarded a further 11 regional lines under a public service obligation.

  8. 2004

    After complicated negotiations, in 2004 the company Autoexpres Karel Psota, s.r.o., whose managing director Karel Psota Jr. is also one of the managing directors of Znojemská dopravní společnost, acquired the area of the former Znojmo dairies, now Autocentrum. In February 2005 the adaptation of the office space in the administrative building was finished, and all administrative staff including the management moved to this new location at Dobšická 6 in Znojmo at the beginning of March 2005.

  9. 2005

    With its transport performance in 2005 our company was among the significant carriers of the region. In total the company carried 1,418,000 passengers in 2005 and drove 2,923,000 kilometres. In 2005 therefore 158,000 more passengers were carried than in 2004. The year 2005 was also significant for our company because we returned to the alternative use of fuels, and at the end of the year we took over from an authorised company two Karosa line buses converted to CNG, i.e. natural gas drive. In April and June 2006 two more CNG line buses were added to them. Although the existing premises are sufficient from the service point of view, because of the growth of the fleet the parking areas in the Agrostav site were becoming rather cramped. We therefore dealt with the problem of obtaining further parking areas within an acceptable distance of the bus terminals.

  10. 2006

    Since 2006 the company's buses have gradually received a new yellow-and-white livery, and new digital destination signs were installed on the buses to better inform passengers. The company's long-distance coaches are silver with a distinctive company marking. In 2006 the company joined the nationwide automated seat-reservation system for bus transport AMS bus. Passengers can therefore buy a ticket to any domestic or foreign destination served by a carrier registered in the system at our pre-sale and information office on Rudoleckého street. From 1 July 2006 the company further expanded its subsidised transport by winning the tender for line 440 in the IDS system, and since then also operates city transport in Moravský Krumlov. A detached workplace was set up in the agricultural cooperative area in Moravský Krumlov – Rakšice to provide the operating conditions.

  11. 2008

    In 2008 the company obtained accreditation to operate a training centre for the training and further training of professional drivers of groups C, D, E with classrooms in Znojmo and Kolín.

  12. 2009

    In 2009 the company obtained the ISO 9001 quality management certificate.

  13. 2010

    In 2010 the company's largest construction investment was put into operation – its own natural gas filling station for its own needs and for the public. After winning a tender we began operating city bus transport in Znojmo (801–809) for the period from 1 January 2010 to 31 December 2022. At the same time a new information and advance-sale centre with modern interiors was put into operation on Rudoleckého street 28 in Znojmo. In the same year the whole Znojmo region was newly integrated into IDS JMK (the Integrated Transport System of the South Moravian Region), where lines were tendered and our company began to provide lines from Znojmo to Brno (108), the whole Vranov area (816, 817, 830, 831), Hevlín (820), around Moravský Krumlov (432, 441, 442, 443, 444, 445) and also partial lines to Tavíkovice, Trstěnice (810, 811, 812, 813, 814, 815) for the period from 1 July 2010 to 12 December 2015 (subsequently extended to 2017 because of a poor tender).

  14. 2012

    The tourist train in Znojmo has been operating daily from May to September.

  15. 2016

    Start of operation of the Vinobus for VOC Znojmo. A great milestone for the company at the end of the year was, after years of negotiations, the purchase of its own premises on Vídeňská / Hakenova street, the former road-maintenance company (Colas) site.

  16. 2017

    In 2017 the company moved – its administration and the parking of all buses to new premises, where the reconstruction of changing rooms and facilities for drivers and the relocation of the diesel + AdBlue filling station also began. After re-tendering of the lines our company, from December, defended the provision of lines from Znojmo to Brno (108), the whole Vranov area (816, 817, 830, 831) and also newly obtained lines from (LAA) Hevlín to Brno (104) and Blansko (815)

  17. 2021

    In 2021 demolition work and reconstruction of the service premises and workshops began, including the electrical installation, enlargement of inadequate service pits, enlargement of doors, installation of ventilation, etc. At the end of the year the "workshop", i.e. the equipment from the original premises in the Agrostav site, where bus service had taken place since 1994, was also moved. In 2021 the company won a tender in the Mikulov area within IDS JMK, where from mid-December for about 9 years we provide regional transport with 20 new low-floor buses (unfortunately already in IDS JMK colours).

  18. 2022

    Since the beginning of the year the company has been fully moved into its own newly reconstructed premises at Hakenova 21 in Znojmo, which house the administration, service facilities, facilities for drivers and the parking of city, regional and long-distance buses including the tourist trains. At present the main activity of the company remains road passenger transport, repair work, the operation of two public fuel stations and a public CNG filling station. It also runs a driving school for groups B, C, D, E and an accredited training centre for professional drivers of groups C, D, E.

A reliable supplier of quality services for everyone since 1990.
