Group A1

entitles you to drive light motorcycles with a maximum output of 11 kW and an engine displacement of up to 125 cc, and tricycles with a maximum output of 15 kW. The minimum age of the applicant is 16, so the consent of a legal guardian is required.

Group B

entitles you to drive:

  • passenger motor vehicles whose maximum permissible weight does not exceed 3,500 kg and with no more than 8 seats besides the driver's seat; a trailer with a maximum permissible weight not exceeding 750 kg may be attached to such a motor vehicle
  • tractors and self-propelled work machines whose maximum permissible weight does not exceed 3,500 kg (so-called small tractors...)
  • vehicle combinations consisting of a motor vehicle under point 1) or 2) and a trailer, if the maximum permissible weight of the combination does not exceed 3,500 kg and the maximum permissible weight of the trailer does not exceed the kerb weight of the motor vehicle, from 18 years of age.

 

Group C

entitles you to drive motor vehicles whose maximum permissible weight exceeds 3,500 kg, designed for carrying no more than 8 persons besides the driver, to which a trailer with a maximum permissible weight not exceeding 750 kg may be attached.

Group C also includes group T and C1, which is limited to a vehicle weight of up to 7,500 kg.

The age for obtaining the group C licence is normally 21. However, our training centre has an exemption for car mechanic apprentices, who can obtain the licence at 18.

Groups C+E

For holders of group C, which entitles them to drive combinations with a semi-trailer or trailer with a maximum permissible weight exceeding 750 kg. The entitlement also applies to groups B+E and C1+E and, if the driver holds group D, also to group D+E. 

Combined training of groups B + C

With combined training of groups B + C you can obtain the licence for both groups within one training cycle. The advantage is shared theory lessons for both groups, followed by practical driving lessons, all concluded with a joint exam.

At the exam only one test is completed; if it is passed, an oral exam on vehicle operation and maintenance follows, and if the candidate also passes this part, a practical driving test is carried out – both in a group B vehicle and in a group C vehicle.

Our company is accredited to operate a training centre for continuing professional qualification of drivers (groups C, E).

Regular training for continuing professional qualification of drivers in groups: C, C+E
We provide both initial and periodic training. Training is carried out with our own vehicles. Please send applications for training via the link on this page.

Training for group A1

Training for group A1 includes: 26 hours of theory, 13 hours of driving and 6 hours of practical vehicle maintenance and first-aid training.

Training for group B

Training for group B includes: 36 hours of theory, 28 hours of driving and 6 hours of practical vehicle maintenance and first-aid training.

Instructor: Miroslav HRSTKA

Training for group C

Training for group C includes: 38 hours of theory, 18 hours of driving and 8 hours of practical vehicle maintenance and first-aid training.

Instructor: Miroslav HRSTKA

Training for group C+E

Training for group C+E includes: 14 hours of theory, 8 hours of driving and 4 hours of practical maintenance and first-aid training.

Instructor: Miroslav HRSTKA

Other fees – Znojmo municipal office

The course price does not include the fees for taking the exams and retakes at the Znojmo municipal office, which are CZK 700.

Fees for repeated exams:

  • repeated theory tests: CZK 100
  • repeated driving tests: CZK 400
  • repeated exam on vehicle operation and maintenance: CZK 200

The fees are payable at the cashier's desk of the Znojmo municipal office. Without payment, the documents confirming a successful exam will not be issued.
